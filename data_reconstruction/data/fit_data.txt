This low eccentricity is a hallmark of hot Jupiters, which are expected to have tidally circularized over time.
The modest differences in the argument of periastron (ω) and time of periastron passage (Tₚ) between the two datasets arise from the near-circular nature of the orbit. When e ≈ 0, there is no well-defined "closest approach," rendering ω and Tₚ partially degenerate with the overall phase of the velocity curve. The optimizer can trade off these parameters while preserving the same physical radial velocity signal. The linear parameters A and B (which encode the amplitude and phase of the sinusoid) remain consistent with the observed K values in both cases.
